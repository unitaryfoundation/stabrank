# research/n4_rank6: the rank-6 exclusion of |N>^4

The pipeline behind `bounds/N-m4-lower-7.json` (chi(N^4) >= 7 at the
attested tier; with the Lean rank-7 witness, chi(N^4) = 7): every rank-6
decomposition of |N>^4
has, along qutrits 1, 2 at the base point (2, 2), four, five or six
visible terms (Fact B of PR #86), so its base is a full 4-, 5- or
6-multiset of |N>^2 and the invisible terms lie on the 16 affine flats of
F_3^2 missing (2, 2). Stage A6 runs the 37,201,212 full 6-covers of
distinct independent states through the compiled stage A kernel; stage B6
the 259,655 G_2 orbit representatives of the dependent 6-sets through the
fresh-term filter and the reference matcher; stage C6 the 321,553
representatives of the repeated 6-multisets through the block paths;
stage (beta') the 54,488 representatives of the full 5-multisets with the
invisible term on each of the 16 flats and stage (gamma) the 505
representatives of the full 4-multisets with each of the 136 flat
multisets through `invisible3.InvisibleMatcher3`. The design and its
measurements are `docs/notes/n4_rank6_design.md`; the argument, the case
split, the controls and the soundness checklist are
`docs/notes/n4_rank6_exclusion.md`.

Files

| file | role |
|---|---|
| `common.py` | the cell (n_1 = 2, base point (2, 2), orbit N), the 16 flats and 136 flat multisets, hashing, the loaders of the census, the representative lists and the partition, the exact re-decision of a hit |
| `invisible3.py` | `InvisibleMatcher3.run_one` (stage beta', one invisible term on a given flat) and `run_two` (stage gamma, two invisible terms on a given flat multiset): the shape table of the visible terms, the exact points, the one-fresh-term scan, the rank-2 residual scan with the same-state and cancelling pairs, blocks and coefficient families |
| `filters6.py` | the fresh-term filter of stage B6 (kappa 1): the exact first-slice solutions of a dependent 6-set from three compiled dense solves on the translate-basis projections |
| `stages.py` | the per-item runs of stages A6 (the kernel directly), B6 (filter, then the reference) and C6, and the candidate cap |
| `driver.py` | `lists --write`, `control-lists`, `sample STAGE`, `partition`, `control-planted`, `control-witness`, `control-orbit`, `control-m3` |
| `reps_N.json` | the orbit-representative lists (B6 with kappa, C6, k5, k4) as base-360 codes, hashed |
| `partition.json` | the batches, hashed, with the census and list hashes |
| `batch.py K` | one batch (`--resume`, `--max-seconds`, exit 2 on `DECOMPOSITION FOUND`, exit 1 on any undecided run) |
| `aggregate.py` | the certificate-side check and the batch manifest (`--dry-run --partial` during the run, `--recheck 2 --recheck-seed 20260925` at the end) |
| `degenerate6.py`, `probe6.py`, `census6_full.py`, `invisible3_probe.py`, `orbits5.py`, `diag/` | the design note's prototypes and measurements |
| `results/` | `census6_N_full.json` (the 6-cover census per pivot pair, the covers regenerated by the kernel), the design records, `control_*.json`, `rates.json`, `sample_*.json`, the 1,057 pod records `batch_K.json` with their `batch_K.log`, the `--no-native` replays under `nonative/`, and `logs/` (the laptop logs and the pod's aggregate, chain, and replay logs) |
| `batch_manifest.json` | the aggregate's manifest of the 1,057 records with their SHA-256, what `bounds/N-m4-lower-7.json` attests to; `verify_challenge/cert_n_m4_rank6_attested.py` is its certificate |

The pod run (2026-09-25 to 2026-09-26, 121.5 CPU-hours, the 23-batch
rerun after the stage B6 filter fix of PR #153, the two seeded rechecks,
and the `--no-native` replays of batches 53 and 764) is the exclusion
note's section 8.
`STABRANK_NO_NATIVE=1` (or `batch.py K --no-native`) keeps the 6-cover
kernel, the stage A matcher and the dense solve in the Python references;
the deterministic hash of a batch record does not depend on the path (the
kernel-run, filter-path, candidate and raw feature-zero counts sit outside
it).
