# research/s5_rank5

Stored outputs and certificate support for chi(S^5) >= 6.

## Attested note (full text)

The bound files carry a shortened version of this note (the schema caps it at 2,000 characters).

The stored outputs are the census (4,096 chunks of 3 or 4 pivot pairs: pairs, covers, the prefixes skipped because the third letter lies in span(t, u, a) modulo P1, counters), the level-5 kappa = 0 lifts (128 chunks over the 2,574 bases of results/b4_reps_v2.json) and the kappa = 1 family brute force (35 files, one per base of results/b4_reps_v1.json, each with its 243 first-option ranges). The certificate verifies every stored output by hash and recomputes in full: the letters, the symmetry group and the pivot pairs, the rank-4 census and its closure (the 2,160 four-term decompositions of |S>^3), the premises chi(S^3) >= 4 and chi(S^5) >= 5 exactly (so a decomposition with four or fewer terms fails the run), Theorem F.3 exactly (no five-term decomposition of |S>^5 with a product term, over the three representatives of |S>^4 and the complete four-qutrit letter table), the closure of the stored covers (2,013,120 in 154 orbits) with every representative confirmed over Z[omega], the P2 recheck of every one-prime skip, every lift from level 3 to level 4, the 9,936 four-term decompositions of |S>^4, the 756 product-pattern completions, the span scan of all 7,439,040 four-qutrit letters and the assembly of the 2,609 level-4 bases (compared with the stored lists, the 2,574 independent ones confirmed exactly), and checks that the stored level-5 results cover every base with nothing found. Whole work units re-run from the seed ('recomputed' = 3): one census chunk of three pivot pairs with stored covers re-enumerated from scratch with fresh functionals and compared cover for cover and skip for skip, and two kappa = 0 lift chunks (about 40 bases) re-lifted; on top of these, three 100-prefix slices (two around stored covers), 120 further bases and 4 of the 8,505 family ranges are recomputed. Every modular decision in the work is exact by Hadamard's bound (minors of norm at most 6^6 against P of order 2e9). Completeness rests on the stored outputs and on the committed code (research/s5_rank5), which regenerates any chunk, lift chunk or family range (census.py run 5 OUT K 4096; lift45.py OUT K 128 --kappa0-only; lift45_fam.py OUT B K 243).
