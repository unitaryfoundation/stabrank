Participate

Run it yourself, or point a coding agent at it.

git clone https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/stabrank
cd stabrank
# describe your decomposition in bounds/mybound.json
make fit BOUND=bounds/mybound.json ARGS=--write
make verify BOUND=bounds/mybound.json

fit solves for the exact coefficients, or tells you no exact combination of your terms reproduces the target. verify rebuilds the identity in exact arithmetic. If it passes, open a pull request adding the file and the leaderboard picks it up on the next build.

A bound the pipeline cannot check is recorded as cited and never takes a record.

Notes

With the QPG cat witness bounds/qubit_H-m6-upper-6.json this settles χ(H^6) = 6. The argument (docs/notes/h6_rank5_exclusion.md) depends on: (1) property P of PR #87, computed by research/constructions/two_qubit_slice.py (501 s of tables, declared here and not re-run): every four-qubit slice of a rank-5 decomposition of |H⟩⊗6 along a 2 + 4 bipartition has all five terms nonzero, since a four-term slice would be one of the 30 rank-4 decompositions of |H⟩⊗4 and none extends. (2) The all-visible slice lemma (note, section 2): a term is a plane term along at most 7 of the 20 triples, so some triple has at most one plane term and a base point where all five terms are nonzero. (3) The slice structure lemma of PR #87, which makes the matcher's option sets exhaustive. (4) Exactness: all amplitudes lie in Q(zeta_16), and 65521 and 2013265921 are 1 mod 16, so the modular kernels list supersets of the true covers and matches, and every candidate cover and every hit is re-decided mod 2013265921 and in floating point (research/h6_rank5/common.decide_terms), stored hits again by the aggregate. (5) The two positive controls (note, section 5): all 23 classes of rank-4 decompositions of |H⟩⊗4 recovered from the full 4-covers of |H⟩⊗3, and the rank-6 witness recovered from all four of its all-visible bases, distinct and repeated (results/control_*.json). Stage A ran through the compiled kernels cpp/src/cover5.cpp and cpp/src/slice_match.cpp, checked against the Python reference in tests/test_slice_cover.py and cpp/tests/ on the sample, the controls and planted instances; stage A batch 14 is being re-run with STABRANK_NO_NATIVE=1 (Python reference) on the pod and its deterministic hash will be recorded in research/h6_rank5/results/nonative_14.json before this bound is merged. Attested rather than reproduced because no certificate budget re-runs the 73 CPU-hours.
