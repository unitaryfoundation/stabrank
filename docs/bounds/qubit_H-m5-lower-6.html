Participate

Run it yourself, or point a coding agent at it.

git clone https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/stabrank
cd stabrank
# describe your decomposition in bounds/mybound.json
make fit BOUND=bounds/mybound.json ARGS=--write
make verify BOUND=bounds/mybound.json

fit solves for the exact coefficients, or tells you no exact combination of your terms reproduces the target. verify rebuilds the identity in exact arithmetic. If it passes, open a pull request adding the file and the leaderboard picks it up on the next build.

A bound the pipeline cannot check is recorded as cited and never takes a record.

Notes

With the rank-6 witness bounds/qubit_H-m5-upper-6.json (Lean tier) this settles χ(H^5) = 6. The argument (docs/notes/h5_rank5_exclusion.md) depends on: (1) Fact 1, every term full along every qubit: the residual tables of research/constructions/two_qubit_slice.py for the 30 rank-4 decompositions of |H⟩⊗4 have no exact and no stabilizer combination at the ratio tan(π/8)^{+-1} (recomputed, results/tables.json; the completeness of the 30-element list up to symmetry is assumed as in the H^6 bound). (2) Fact 2, at most two absent terms at any two-qubit point: χ(H^3) = 3, bounds/qubit_H-m3-lower-3.json. (3) The flat lemma and the base-point case split of the note: stage α (all visible at 00 or 01) or stage β (four visible, one line term on the other diagonal); the configuration with two line terms on each diagonal is excluded by the same tables for the 6 rank-3 decompositions of |H⟩⊗3 at the ratio tan(π/8)^{+-2} (0 exact combinations, results/tables.json). (4) The slice structure lemma, which makes every option set exhaustive; stage β takes the invisible term present at both coordinate points and absent on the diagonal through the base point (Fact 1) and assumes nothing about the visible terms' flats. (5) The H^6 census (research/h6_rank5/results/kernel_census.json) and degenerate list (degenerate_covers_v2.json, cancel-at-base multisets included) reused by hash; the stage β list beta_covers.json re-enumerated by the certificate. (6) Exactness: amplitudes in Q(zeta_16), 65521 and 2013265921 are 1 mod 16, modular kernels list supersets, every candidate and hit re-decided mod 2013265921 and in floating point; a block or residual the matcher cannot decide raises and fails the batch (none did). (7) Controls (results/control_*.json): the rank-6 witness from its 24 all-visible pair bases, planted stage β instances of five kinds, the rank-4 decompositions of |H⟩⊗4 along a qubit pair.
