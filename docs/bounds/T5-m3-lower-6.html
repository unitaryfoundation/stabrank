Run it yourself, or point a coding agent at it.

git clone https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/stabrank
cd stabrank
# describe your decomposition in bounds/mybound.json
make fit BOUND=bounds/mybound.json ARGS=--write
make verify BOUND=bounds/mybound.json

fit solves for the exact coefficients, or tells you no exact combination of your terms reproduces the target. verify rebuilds the identity in exact arithmetic. If it passes, open a pull request adding the file and the leaderboard picks it up on the next build.

A bound the pipeline cannot check is recorded as cited and never takes a record.

Notes

χ(|T5⟩⊗2) = 5 (bounds/T5-m2-upper-5.json at the Lean tier and bounds/T5-m2-lower-5.json, re-derived exactly by the k = 1..4 censuses the certificate runs). Every amplitude of |T5⟩ is nonzero, so a rank-5 decomposition of |T5⟩⊗3 sliced at the third ququint gives, at each of the five slice values, a decomposition of |T5⟩⊗2 with at most five terms; χ(|T5⟩⊗2) = 5 forces every slice of every term to be nonzero and the five slices pairwise non-parallel, so each slice is a 5-set with |T5⟩⊗2 in its span, and the census lists exactly one such set up to the unitary symmetry group: the lines x + y = c with phase w^(3c x^2 - 3c^2 x), fixed by the group. The coefficient of each line in |T5⟩⊗2 is unique, so a lifted term is determined by its slice pattern c(z) and equals the restriction of |T5⟩⊗3 to {x + y = c(z)}. research/t5_m3_lift/lift.py decides over Q(zeta_5) with rational arithmetic whether five such pattern vectors are stabilizer states forming a bijection at every z: of the 3,125 patterns, 25 survive the exact prunes (the affine c(z) = c0 + d z, the only ones with a flat support) and none is a stabilizer state, since the phase has the cubic part (1 + d^3) z^3 + 3 d z x^2 - 3 d^2 z^2 x on that plane. Hence χ(|T5⟩⊗3) ≥ 6, and by projection monotonicity χ(|T5⟩⊗4) ≥ 6 (bounds/T5-m4-lower-6.json). No invisible or coincident term arises at exact rank; excluding rank 6 at m = 3 would need them and is not claimed. Controls (research/t5_m3_lift/results/, docs/notes/t5_m3_lift_design.md section 4): the basis of |T5⟩ lifts to exactly the board's five sectors, the ten rank-3 decompositions of |T5⟩ do not lift, planted products |T5⟩⊗2 ⊗ β are recovered as the only lift, the qutrit T3 and N cells behave as the board says, and random stabilizer states are recognized. With bounds/T5-m3-upper-15.json the cell is 6 ≤ χ(|T5⟩⊗3) ≤ 15.
