Participate

Run it yourself, or point a coding agent at it.

git clone https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/stabrank
cd stabrank
# describe your decomposition in bounds/mybound.json
make fit BOUND=bounds/mybound.json ARGS=--write
make verify BOUND=bounds/mybound.json

fit solves for the exact coefficients, or tells you no exact combination of your terms reproduces the target. verify rebuilds the identity in exact arithmetic. If it passes, open a pull request adding the file and the leaderboard picks it up on the next build.

A bound the pipeline cannot check is recorded as cited and never takes a record.

Notes

With the Lean rank-8 witness bounds/H3-m4-upper-8.json this moves the cell to 7 ≤ χ(H3^4) ≤ 8. Every rank-6 decomposition of |H3⟩⊗4 has four, five, or six terms visible at the base point (0, 0) along qutrits 1, 2 (Fact B of PR #86: no two-qutrit slice of a rank-5 or rank-6 decomposition of |H3⟩⊗4 has exactly three nonzero terms, and χ(H3^2) = 3 leaves at least three), so the base is a full 4-, 5-, or 6-multiset of |H3⟩⊗2, one per orbit of the unitary symmetry group of |H3⟩⊗2 (order 32), and the invisible terms lie on the 16 flats of F3^2 missing (0, 0): stages A6 (the 36,368,678 full 6-covers of distinct independent states), B6 (the dependent 6-sets), C6 (the repeated 6-multisets), β' (the full 5-multisets with one invisible term on each of the 16 flats), and γ (the full 4-multisets with each of the 136 flat multisets, the pairs cancelling at a common first point carried explicitly). The lists rest on the attested rank-5 lists of bounds/H3-m4-lower-6.json and on the 6-cover census; the facts of PR #86 are declared, not re-run. Exactness: amplitudes in Q(omega_3, √ 3), both primes 1 mod 12, every modular kernel a superset, every candidate and hit re-decided mod 2013265921 and numerically. Controls (research/h3_m4_rank6/results/control_*.json): planted decompositions of every kind for every stage, flat, and flat multiset, the orbit lemma, the rank-4 decomposition of |H3⟩⊗3, the lists and census by hash, the rank-8 witness recorded. The design, case split, and cost table are docs/notes/h3_m4_rank6_design.md. Attested because no certificate budget re-runs the pod hours.
