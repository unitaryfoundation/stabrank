Verification

verified   18 stabilizer terms reproduce |qubit_H>^{ot 9}, checked symbolically

Implied per-copy exponent γ ≤ 0.4633 against a published 0.3962, no improvement.

Compute

1 run; 0.01 CPU-hours; 0.01 h wall-clock; on Apple silicon laptop, one core; direct construction, no search.

Notes

χ(|H⟩⊗9) ≤ 18, the first entry for this cell; exponent log_2(18)/9 = 0.4633, above the published log_2(3)/4. Construction: the nine-term glued |cat10⟩ of Qassim, Pashayan, and Gosset (as restated by Kissinger, van de Wetering, and Vilmart) projected onto |0⟩ and onto |1⟩ on the tenth qubit gives |cat9⟩ and |cat_9^→ = (|T⟩⊗9 - |Tperp⟩⊗9)/√ 2 in nine terms each, and |T⟩⊗9 = (|cat9⟩ + |cat_9^→)/√ 2. Every other route through the same machinery (gluing cat_6 with cat_5, the 4-to-3 partial decomposition times the board's m=5 witness, the products T^6 x T^3 and T^7 x T^2) also gives 18 distinct terms; the five routes use 81 distinct stabilizer states spanning a 66-dimensional space, and greedy and randomised pruning inside that pool found nothing shorter than 18 (research/constructions/kvv_cat_m9.py). Built here and verified in exact arithmetic.
