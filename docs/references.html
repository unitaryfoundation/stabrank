Participate

Run it yourself, or point a coding agent at it.

git clone https://github.com/unitaryfoundation/stabrank
cd stabrank
# describe your decomposition in bounds/mybound.json
make fit BOUND=bounds/mybound.json ARGS=--write
make verify BOUND=bounds/mybound.json

fit solves for the exact coefficients, or tells you no exact combination of your terms reproduces the target. verify rebuilds the identity in exact arithmetic. If it passes, open a pull request adding the file and the leaderboard picks it up on the next build.

A bound the pipeline cannot check is recorded as cited and never takes a record.

  1. Bravyi, Sergey, Gosset, David. Improved Classical Simulation of Quantum Circuits Dominated by Clifford Gates. Physical Review Letters, vol. 116, p. 250501, 2016 · arXiv:1601.07601 · DOI
  2. Bravyi, Sergey, Smith, Graeme, Smolin, John A.. Trading Classical and Quantum Computational Resources. Physical Review X, vol. 6, p. 021043, 2016 · arXiv:1506.01396 · DOI
    Introduces stabilizer rank and the six-copy decomposition of the qubit H-type state.
  3. Kocia, Lucas, Sarovar, Mohan. Improved Simulation of Quantum Circuits by Fewer Gaussian Eliminations. Physical Review A, vol. 103, p. 022603, 2021 · arXiv:2003.01130
    Qutrit stabilizer-rank numerics; records chi(T3^3) as 8? and flags the search as possibly unconverged.
  4. Qassim, Hammam, Pashayan, Hakop, Gosset, David. Improved upper bounds on the stabilizer rank of magic states. Quantum, vol. 5, p. 606, 2021 · arXiv:2106.07740 · DOI
    Contracted cat states; source of the log2(3)/4 exponent.
  5. Labib, Farrokh. Stabilizer rank and higher-order Fourier analysis. Quantum, vol. 6, p. 645, 2022 · arXiv:2107.10551 · DOI
    Omega(n) exact-rank lower bound at every prime p; the p=3 magic state is the T3 orbit.
  6. Lovitz, Benjamin, Steffan, Vincent. New Techniques for Bounding Stabilizer Rank. Quantum, vol. 6, p. 692, 2022 · arXiv:2110.07781 · DOI
    Prop. 3.7 bounds exact rank from the field of definition of stabilizer coordinates, over Q(i).
  7. Peleg, Shir, Shpilka, Amir, Volk, Ben Lee. Lower Bounds on Stabilizer Rank. Quantum, vol. 6, p. 652, 2022 · arXiv:2106.03214 · DOI
    Subset-sum route; needs amplitudes attaining different values across Hamming-weight layers.
  8. Mehraban, Saeed, Tahmasbi, Mehrdad. Quadratic Lower Bounds on the Approximate Stabilizer Rank. 2024 · arXiv:2305.10277
    Omega~(n^2) for approximate rank, which carries to exact rank.
  9. Labib, Farrokh, Russo, Vincent. Stabilizer rank bounds for magic-state orbits. 2026 · arXiv:2605.28586
    Per-copy exponents for the four qutrit orbits; the baselines this challenge is measured against.
